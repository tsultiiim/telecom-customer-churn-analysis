Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

Project Overview
A business analytics project using the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset to identify the key drivers of customer attrition and provide actionable business recommendations.
The project combines Python for data analysis and visualization with SQL for validating key business queries.

Business Problem
Customer churn reduces long-term revenue and customer lifetime value.
This project aims to answer:
Why are customers leaving?
Which customer segments are most at risk?
How much revenue is lost due to churn?
What actions can improve customer retention?

Dataset
IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset
7,043 customers
21 customer attributes

Tools Used
Python
Pandas
NumPy
Matplotlib
SQL (MySQL)
Jupyter Notebook

Key Findings
Overall churn rate: 26.54%
Month-to-month contracts showed the highest churn.
Electronic Check customers had the highest churn among payment methods.
Fiber Optic customers exhibited the highest churn among internet services.
Customers within their first year were the most likely to churn.
Customer churn accounted for 17.83% of historical revenue.
High-risk customer profiles were identified using combinations of contract type, payment method, internet service, and tenure.
